03
The Solution
Three mechanics that transform the experience.

Each mechanic is engineered to counter a specific drop-off reason — delivering progress, insight, and genuine delight.

⚡
Mechanic 01 · Variable Reward
XP + Live Energy Score
Every answer earns XP. A live "Home Energy Score" climbs in real time — users see the number grow with each tap, creating an irresistible feedback loop.
🏠
Mechanic 02 · Narrative Context
Room-by-Room Journey
"Now let's head to your kitchen…" — the camera flies through each room as questions appear. It's a guided home tour, not an interrogation.
💡
Mechanic 03 · Immediate Payoff
In-Survey Energy Tips
After key answers, a contextual energy tip appears immediately. "Your home size suggests 18% heating overhead — here's what to do." Value delivered in the moment, not just at the end.
